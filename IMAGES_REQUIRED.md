## Summary

- **Required:** 6 packages + 53 hotels = **59 images** (mandatory for polished demo)
- **Optional:** 10 destination tiles
- **Already present:** `default.jpg` (generic fallback)

Missing images automatically fall back to `default.jpg` via the `onerror` handler in templates, so partial coverage is fine — hotels without their specific image will just show the default.

## Quick bulk-download tip

If you have `wget` or `curl` on your local machine, you can grab one image per filename fast by searching Unsplash and copying the download URL. Example:

```bash
cd assets/images
curl -L -o taj_udaipur.jpg "https://images.unsplash.com/photo-XXXX?w=800&h=500&fit=crop"
```

Or, download any batch of 60+ hotel/resort photos and rename them to the filenames above — the images don't have to match the exact hotel since this is a demo app.
